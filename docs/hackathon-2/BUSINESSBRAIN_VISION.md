# BusinessBrain Vision

Presentation-only future view at `/vision`.

The page is deliberately separated from the current product and from the
guided jury demo. It visualises how BusinessBrain could develop step by step:

1. Knowledge system — the confirmed product core available today.
2. Business assistant — future interpretation and proposal support.
3. Digital business brain — a future orientation system connecting confirmed
   company knowledge, external signals and human decisions.

## Boundaries

Every future capability carries a visible `VISION` badge. The page repeatedly
states that BusinessBrain:

- never makes business decisions on its own;
- never publishes content on its own;
- never changes company knowledge on its own;
- only proposes traceable actions;
- always leaves the decision to a human.

The block adds no AI logic, prompts, backend, database, Supabase integration,
agents, automation, persistence or mutations. It does not alter the existing
knowledge core or the guided jury demo.

## Visual journey

- future-labelled hero and today's confirmed foundation;
- responsive three-phase product evolution;
- signal → context → proposal → human decision flow;
- grouped future views for digital presence, customer voice, market movement,
  content/action proposals and daily orientation;
- an explicit human-control constitution and a credibility-focused closing.

## Discovery

The route is public so investors and entrepreneurs can open it directly. It is
also linked from `More modules`, while remaining outside the primary jury tour.
